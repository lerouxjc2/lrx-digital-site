Case study

First Choice Blinds SA

Custom outdoor patio blinds and solar screens. Johannesburg, Pretoria and 150 km around.

The challenge

First Choice Blinds SA was a brand-new company with a good product and no way for customers to find it. There was no website, no ads and no social following, in a market where established competitors were already advertising.

What I built

  • Website from the ground up. A five-page site that presents the product range, the up-to-5-year guarantee and the service area clearly. Every page pushes toward a free on-site quote.
  • Built for enquiries, not just looks. A quote form that sends straight to WhatsApp, click-to-call buttons, and business hours and coverage visible up front.
  • Found on Google. Search-friendly page titles and descriptions, Google Search Console verification, a fuller Google Business Profile and social share previews.
  • Meta ads that bring in quotes. Researched competitors’ ads in the Meta Ad Library, then built a WhatsApp enquiry campaign.
  • Tested and adjusted. Tried a qualifying-question WhatsApp greeting. When it cut enquiries, I switched it off and went back to what worked.
  • Google Ads and social media. Set up Google Ads for high-intent searches and designed Facebook and Instagram posts from scratch to keep the pages active.

The results

R5.75Cost per conversation
R932Total ad spend

In 30 days (29 Aug to 27 Sep 2026), one image-ad campaign reached 49,676 people with 59,168 impressions and started 162 WhatsApp conversations with potential customers, at an average of R5.75 each. Every one of those conversations lands straight on the owner’s phone, ready to be turned into a quote.
